Simon

How to Play Classic Simon:
  • Start: Press the start button and then the flashing green light to start the game.
  • Watch & Listen: Simon flashes one of four colored lights (red, blue, green, yellow) and makes a sound.
  • Repeat: Press the corresponding colored pads in the same order.
  • Progression: If you are correct, Simon adds a new color to the sequence, becoming longer and faster.
  • Game Over: If you press the wrong color or take too long, the game makes a failure sound and displays your score.
